About

Composer &
Producer

Ian Chung is a South Korean composer and songwriter who brings a unique perspective through a blend of neo-classical sensibility and acoustic pop warmth.

Passionate about creating music that evokes emotion and connects with people on a personal level, he explores the intersection of different genres and pushes creative boundaries.

His goal is simple — to make music that speaks to the heart, finds beauty in life's quieter moments, and reminds listeners that their story is worth telling.
